# The first passage

Days 1 to 2 of the plan: one auditionable fork with a delayed payoff. One pulse, one choir fragment, one answering voice; a conventional continuation and one that withholds the answer and returns it in another voice after the human's cue. Two renders, a notes file with keep or rework and the recorded decision.

The experiment is owner-local and needs SuperCollider and ears; it may be built with sclang and NRT scripts before any Rust exists. Its result gates the second wave of tasks. Scope discipline from the design of record, section 6: no SQLite, no MCP, no generalized schemas, no restart recovery during these two days; label anything shown as "Experiment; recording real, session history incomplete."
